# Master's Thesis — AI Overreliance

Experimental project developed as part of a master's thesis on
AI overreliance and human decision-making.

## Research

The project aims to quantitatively investigate how AI-generated
recommendations influence human decision-making, particularly in
situations where the AI recommendation is incorrect.

The study will examine factors such as:

- respondent knowledge,
- time pressure,
- AI-declared confidence,
- respondent confidence,
- demographic characteristics,
- and the quality of the final decision.

A central part of the planned experiment is a controlled decision-making
environment in which participants make an initial decision, receive an
AI recommendation, and can subsequently revise their decision.

## Project status

Early research and experiment design phase.

The research protocol and experimental design are currently being
developed and will be refined following methodological consultation.
